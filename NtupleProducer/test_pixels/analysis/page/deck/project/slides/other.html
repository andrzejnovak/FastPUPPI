We looked for any other channel where better L1 flavour tagging pays. Bars to scale (0 to 100%), share of events in the generator-level acceptance kept by L1. Vector-boson fusion (VBF) Higgs production has two forward jets with a large separation; the added seed requires a looser forward pair plus two flavour-tagged central jets at about 2 kHz extra. VBF H to bb: 51% with the planned menu, 56% with the NN seed, 61% with pixel tags. VBF H to cc: 58, 61, 62%. Boosted gluon-fusion Higgs (Higgs pT above 200 GeV) and Z(to neutrinos)H (missing transverse momentum above the usual 150 GeV cut) are already kept at 96 to 100% by the planned single-jet, HT and missing-momentum seeds, so L1 is not where they lose events. Charm tagging also improves with pixels (backup), but the VBF H to cc trigger barely moves. Figures in backup.
