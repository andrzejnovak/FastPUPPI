15 / 16 (+11 backup)

We looked for any channel where better L1 flavour tagging pays. VBF H to bb gains most: the planned menu keeps 51% of events in acceptance, a looser VBF jet pair plus two b-tagged central jets at about 2 kHz extra keeps 56% with today’s NN and 61% with pixel tags. VBF H to cc gains about 3 to 4 points, with pixel and NN tags about equal. Boosted Higgs and Z(nu nu)H are already kept by the planned single-jet, HT and missing-momentum seeds, so L1 is not their bottleneck. Pixel-based versions of these seeds face the same readout-chip limit as HH to 4b; the NN captures part of each gain. Figures in backup.
