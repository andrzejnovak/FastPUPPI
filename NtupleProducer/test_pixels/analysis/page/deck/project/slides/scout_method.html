The scouting test in detail

•

Selection

4 leading jets above 20 GeV, HT > 150 GeV · two Higgs candidates paired by the smallest mass difference · elliptical window around (124, 114) GeV from the signal

•

Fit

binned four-jet mass in categories of 4 and 3 tagged jets · expected (Asimov) significance, statistics only · κλ shapes from generator mHH

•

Background

QCD in p̂T bins 30–470 GeV (140 000 events with L1 objects) · private Pythia sample filtered for ≥ 4 b quarks (about 3 000 events through the full simulation) for genuine 4b

•

Two background shape models

tagged events per bin, or untagged shape × tagged fraction (keeps the mass binning when few tagged events exist)

•

Limits

genuine 4b in the window from 16–19 simulated events · pileup b-jets unlabelled · no tt̄ · offline-quality jets could not test 4 tags (no 4b background event passed)

Backup · Scouting method

backup 6 / 11

Signal: SM gluon-fusion HH to 4b, 3000 fb-1. The triggered stream uses offline-quality b-tag probabilities per jet (b 84%, c 12%, light 1%). Scouting covers only crossings the HT seeds reject, so the two streams are disjoint and combined in one likelihood. The 4b-enriched sample: Pythia 8 hard QCD, pT-hat 30 to 300 GeV with a bias towards higher pT-hat and compensating weights, filtered before detector simulation for at least four b quarks above 15 GeV. Code: scout4b.py, scout_kin.py.
