Gluon fusion makes Higgs pairs through two diagrams that interfere destructively. Only the triangle contains the Higgs self-coupling, so changing kappa_lambda changes how much the two cancel, and the cancellation matters most near threshold. The chart shows this per 50 GeV bin of the generated Higgs-pair mass: grey is where the Standard-Model events are (they peak at 350 to 450 GeV), red is where the information about kappa_lambda is (signal-only measure: the squared derivative of the rate with respect to kappa_lambda, divided by the rate). 77% of the information is below 400 GeV (78% with finer bins). Cross-section: 36.7 fb (gluon fusion, 14 TeV) times BR(H to bb) squared, 0.58 squared = 0.34.
