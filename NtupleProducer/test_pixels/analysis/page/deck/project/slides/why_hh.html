Gluon fusion makes Higgs pairs through two diagrams: a triangle top loop producing an off-shell Higgs that splits into two (this one contains the self-coupling), and a box top loop (no self-coupling). They interfere destructively, and the interference is strongest near threshold, so the low-mass end of the m_HH spectrum carries most of the sensitivity. The information measure here is signal-only: the squared derivative of the rate with respect to kappa_lambda, divided by the rate, per mass bin, at the SM point. Cross-section: 36.7 fb (gluon fusion, 14 TeV) times BR(H to bb) squared, 0.58 squared = 0.34.
