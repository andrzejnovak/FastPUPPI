Robustness checks of route A. The 64-bit word quantises each of the 8 summary numbers to 8 bits, as a trigger board would send them. Hit loss: 10% of pixel clusters dropped at random, tagger retrained. Without the innermost layer: the layer that dominates the readout volume.
