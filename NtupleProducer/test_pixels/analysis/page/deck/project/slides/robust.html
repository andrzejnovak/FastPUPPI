II · Idea A: pixels in L1 · Robustness

10 / 16 (+11 backup)

Robustness checks of the same chain. The 64-bit word quantises each of the 8 summary numbers to 8 bits, as a trigger board would send them. Hit loss: 10% of pixel clusters dropped at random, tagger retrained. Without the innermost layer: the layer that dominates the readout volume. Innermost layer only: one hit per track, the L1 track fixes the line; light-jet tracks then show a median impact parameter of 66 microns instead of 19, because the extrapolated direction is too uncertain and wrong hits cannot be rejected. Not studied: FPGA latency and resources.
