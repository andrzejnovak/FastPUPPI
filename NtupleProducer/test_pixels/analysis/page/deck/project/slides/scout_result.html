Bars are to scale, 0 to 1.25 sigma. “Triggered events only”: the crossings the HT seeds accept, analysed with offline-quality b-tag probabilities. Scouting adds the crossings the HT seeds reject, analysed with the stated tags, in the same likelihood. With L1 jets, perfect tags take 1.07 to 1.10 sigma, +2.5%. With generator-level jets (an idealisation: perfect resolution, no pileup jets) the Higgs mass window halves and the gain grows to +5 to +9%, depending on how the background shape is modelled. Today’s NN tags add nothing: at a mistag low enough to suppress the background, requiring four tags keeps only a few percent of the signal. The absolute significance depends on the background model by up to about 30%; the scouting gain is small in every variant. Caveats: one fixed analysis, not a general bound; the genuine 4b background rests on 16 to 19 simulated events; b-jets from pileup collisions carry no generator label, so the b-rich background is if anything underestimated.
