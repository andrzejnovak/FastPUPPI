Expected significance: how many standard deviations the SM HH signal would stand above background-only, in an idealised counting of the fitted four-jet mass (Asimov data, statistics only, 3000 fb-1). Triggered events: the crossings the HT seeds accept, analysed with offline-quality b-tag probabilities. Scouting adds the crossings the HT seeds reject, in the same likelihood. With L1 jets: 1.070 sigma becomes 1.096 with perfect tags (+2.5%) and stays at 1.070 with the baseline NN tags. With idealised generator-level jets the mass window halves; perfect-tag scouting then gives +5.2% or +9.0% depending on how the background shape is modelled (per-bin tagged events, or untagged shape times tagged fraction). The roughly 1 sigma is for this simplified trigger-object study, not a CMS HH projection; it shifts by up to about 30% between background models, while the scouting gain stays small. Caveats: one fixed analysis, not a general bound; the genuine 4b background rests on 16 to 19 simulated events; b-jets from pileup collisions carry no generator label.
