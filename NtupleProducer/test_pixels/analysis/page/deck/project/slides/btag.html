The figure shows d0 of L1 tracks minus d0 of the same particle reconstructed as a pixel-only track in the software trigger, for tracks above 2 GeV. Widths are robust (1.4826 times the median absolute deviation). Because the reference shares pixel hits with the extended tracks, 35 microns is a residual width, not a truth resolution, but the factor of 15 is the point. The baseline L1 b-tagger is a neural network on up to 16 particle candidates per jet; its tracks come from a five-parameter fit in which d0 is free, but their resolution limits it. Light-jet mistag: the fraction of light-quark and gluon jets wrongly tagged.
