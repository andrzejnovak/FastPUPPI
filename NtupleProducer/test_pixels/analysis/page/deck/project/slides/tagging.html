How to read a tagging curve: each tagger has a threshold; moving it trades b-jet efficiency (x) against the light-jet mistag rate (y, log scale). Lower right is better. At a fixed 1% mistag the baseline hardware NN tags 15% of b-jets, adding displacement information from the outer tracker alone gives 19%, and adding the pixel-extended tracks gives 47%, close to what pixel-only tracks reconstructed in software after L1 give (50%). The offline tagger, on offline jets with full tracking, is there for scale. b jets from HH to 4b, light jets (not b or c, including gluons) from QCD multijet events, L1 jets above 15 GeV, 200 pileup; each “+” curve is a separately trained classifier on the NN scores plus the named features, trained on one half of the events and evaluated on the other.
