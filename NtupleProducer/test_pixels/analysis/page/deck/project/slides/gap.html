Three populations, used throughout. All simulated SM gluon-fusion HH to 4b events: the two HT seeds keep 60%. Those with a Higgs-pair mass below 400 GeV, where most of the self-coupling information is: 36%. Those below 400 GeV that a mock offline selection would actually use (four jets above 30 GeV, four medium or three medium plus one loose b-tags, both Higgs candidates in the mass window): 60%, because the offline selection also prefers hard jets. From here on, “usable low-mass events” means this last group. Why not just lower HT? The multijet background rate rises steeply as HT goes down (backup: rate trade-off): spending 15 kHz more on lower HT thresholds keeps 81% of the usable low-mass events, a b-tagged seed at the same rate 93% (later). The seed names quote offline-equivalent thresholds; the emulator applies HT above 361 and 316 GeV.
