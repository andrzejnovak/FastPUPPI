Glossary

TermMeaning
L1 (Level-1)CMS hardware trigger: 12.5 µs, 40 MHz → 750 kHz (ATLAS: L0, 1 MHz, 10 µs)
HLThigh-level trigger: software farm after L1, stores about 7.5 kHz
Seedone L1 trigger condition, e.g. “HT > 450 GeV”; the menu is the list
HTscalar sum of jet transverse momenta (L1 jets above 30 GeV)
L1 tracksouter-tracker tracks above 2 GeV, found in FPGAs for every crossing
d₀, z₀transverse and longitudinal impact parameter: closest approach to the beam line
Mistagshare of light jets (u, d, s quarks, gluons) wrongly tagged as b
κλHiggs self-coupling divided by its Standard-Model value
Usable eventpasses a mock offline selection: 4 jets, 3–4 b-tags, Higgs mass window
40 MHz scoutingprocessing the L1 objects of every crossing, also those L1 rejects
CROC, ITkPixCMS and ATLAS pixel readout chips, both from the RD53 development
