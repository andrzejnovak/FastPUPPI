Can pixels help the trigger keep low-mass HH→4b?

•

The problem: low-mass HH→4b carries most of the Higgs self-coupling information

the planned hardware trigger keeps only about a third of those events

•

Idea A: add pixel-detector hits to the hardware-trigger b-tag

works in simulation (b-tag efficiency 15% → 47%), but the pixel readout chips cannot deliver hits in time

•

Idea B: analyse the crossings the trigger rejects, in 40 MHz scouting

even with perfect b-tags this adds only 2.5–9% to the expected significance

•

What works: a b-tagged trigger seed built from the tracks CMS already plans

keeps 93% of the analysable low-mass events (instead of 60%) for +16 kHz of trigger rate
