One problem, three routes we tested

I · The problem · Roadmap

2 / 15 (+13 backup)

The roadmap for the talk. First the problem: why low-mass Higgs pairs matter and why the trigger loses them. Then three routes. A: put pixel information into the hardware trigger decision; we test both whether it would help and whether the hardware can deliver it. B: record the collisions the trigger rejects and analyse them later; we test the best possible case, with perfect b-tagging. C: use the b-tagging that the planned hardware trigger already has. All results are CMS Phase-2 simulation with 200 pileup collisions per bunch crossing.
