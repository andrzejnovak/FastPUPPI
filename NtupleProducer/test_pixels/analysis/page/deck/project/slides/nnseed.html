Route C needs no pixels: the outer-tracker tracks and the NN b-tagger are already in the Phase-2 hardware-trigger design. The same seed as before (summed b-scores of the four leading jets plus HT), with the baseline NN scores, at a measured 16.3 kHz on top of the HT seeds keeps 93% of the usable low-mass events. Spending a similar rate (target 15 kHz) on lower HT thresholds keeps 81%. 15 kHz is the rate CMS has shown for a standalone b-tag NN seed, about 2% of the 750 kHz budget (the CMS L1 design menu totals about 450 kHz including a 30% margin). The apparent paradox: the same NN adds nothing in scouting. In the trigger it only needs to reduce the background to a rate we can afford; the offline analysis then applies its 84%-efficient tagger to those events. In scouting there is no offline step, so the trigger-level tag itself would have to separate signal from a background that is 200 000 times larger. Rate-saving figure in backup.
