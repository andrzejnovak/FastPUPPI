The practical lever needs no pixels: the outer-tracker tracks and the NN b-tagger are already in the Phase-2 L1 design. Spending 15 kHz on a seed that combines HT with the summed NN b-scores of the four leading jets keeps 93% of the usable (offline-selectable) events below 400 GeV, against 81% if the same rate goes into lower HT thresholds. “Usable”: four offline jets, 3 to 4 b-tags, both Higgs candidates in the mass window. Kappa_lambda information column: the usable events at all masses. Rates are target values; the measured added rates are in brackets. The CMS L1 Technical Design Report menu totals about 450 kHz of the 750 kHz including a 30% margin. Rate trade-off figure in backup.
