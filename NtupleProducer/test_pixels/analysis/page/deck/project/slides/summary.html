One line per route. Pixel information would make the hardware b-tag much better, but the pixel readout chips have no way to deliver hits before the normal trigger decision. Scouting the rejected collisions adds little even with ideal tagging, because the background with real b-quarks is huge. The b-tagger already planned for the hardware trigger, used in a seed that combines HT with the summed b-scores of the four leading jets, recovers most of the lost low-mass events for 16 kHz of trigger rate: that is the study to take forward.
