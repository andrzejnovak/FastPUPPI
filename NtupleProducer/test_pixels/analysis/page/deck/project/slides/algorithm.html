1

Take each L1 track above 2 GeV

found by the FPGA track finder for every crossing

2

Extrapolate it into the pixel layers

a search window per layer, wide enough for displaced tracks

3

Pick the best-matching hits, refit d₀ and z₀

at least two pixel hits; momentum and direction kept from the L1 track

4

Summarise each jet in 8 numbers

largest impact-parameter significances, displaced-track counts, … → a boosted decision tree, together with the NN scores

II · Idea A: pixels in L1 · Algorithm

7 / 16 (+11 backup)

One selected simulated event. The L1 track (prompt fit, d0 = 0) is extrapolated inward; in each barrel pixel layer we open a window around the prediction and keep the closest clusters; we try combinations and keep the one with the best fit quality, then refit the transverse and longitudinal impact parameters, d0 and z0, with the curvature fixed from the L1 track. Outer-tracker points are drawn along the fitted helix. Each jet then gets 8 numbers: the three largest impact-parameter significances, the number of tracks above 3 and 5 sigma, the sum of significances, the largest impact parameter, and the number of extended tracks. These go into a boosted decision tree together with the existing NN scores.
