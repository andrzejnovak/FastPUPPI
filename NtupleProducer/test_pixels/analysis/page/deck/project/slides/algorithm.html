One track from a selected HH to 4b event with 200 pileup collisions: a 2.3 GeV track in a 70 GeV b-jet. The L1 track was fitted to outer-tracker hits assuming it comes from the beam line (d0 = 0, the dashed line at zero). Left: in each of the four barrel pixel layers we open a window of plus or minus 1.5 mm around the prediction; grey dots are other nearby clusters, black dots the 29 candidates inside the windows. Right: the combination of one hit per layer with the best fit quality is picked (orange), and the refit gives d0 = 498 microns; the software pixel track of the same particle (green circles) gives 532. The vertical axis is stretched ten times. Each jet is then summarised in 8 numbers from its refitted tracks (the largest impact-parameter significances d0/sigma, counts of displaced tracks, and so on) that go into a boosted decision tree together with the existing NN scores. This assumes the hits could be read in time; whether they can is the topic three slides later.
