III · Idea B: scouting · The question

12 / 16 (+11 backup)

CMS plans a system that processes the L1 trigger objects of every crossing, independent of the L1 decision, so analyses can use crossings the trigger rejected, at the price of only having trigger-level objects. (ATLAS has trigger-level analyses on events after its trigger selection; the difference is where the selection happens.) Since pixel hits do not exist outside L1 accepts, we tested an upper bound: give the scouting stream perfect b-tags (generator flavour) and ask how much the rejected crossings add. The card shows why the answer is small: even counting only events where 3 or 4 of the 4 leading jets are true b-jets, the multijet background in the mass window is 200 000 times larger than the signal.
