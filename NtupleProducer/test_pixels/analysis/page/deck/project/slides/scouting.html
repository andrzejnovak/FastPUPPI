III · Route B: scouting · The test

11 / 15 (+13 backup)

Route B skips the trigger decision: CMS plans a system that processes the L1 trigger objects of every crossing, also the rejected ones, so analyses can use them later, with only trigger-level objects. Pixel hits do not exist there either (they leave the chips only after an accept), so we test the most favourable case: give the scouting stream perfect b-tags, from the generator flavour. The selection: the four leading jets, paired into two Higgs candidates, both masses in a window around the signal peak, then a fit of the four-jet mass. In the crossings the HT seeds reject, the window holds about 3 800 signal events with 3 or 4 true b-jets, against about 700 million multijet events that also have 3 or 4 true b-jets, mostly from gluons splitting into b-quark pairs. Ideal tagging cannot remove those. Background: simulated QCD multijets plus a dedicated simulated sample with at least four b quarks.
