Background modelling: a double-counting pitfall

•

Rates come from minimum bias

the right mix of processes, but pileup collisions carry no flavour labels

•

Flavour comes from QCD samples binned in p̂T

p̂T: momentum of the hard scattering · weighted with their cross-sections

•

Below HT 300 GeV, QCD over-predicts by about 1.5×

each QCD event = one hard scatter + 200 pileup collisions that already contain hard scatters (~0.3 per crossing at p̂T 30–50 GeV): a crossing is counted once per hard scatter in it

•

Fix: scale QCD to minimum bias below 300 GeV

above 300 GeV the two agree within 10%

Crossings with at least four L1 jets and L1 HT above 150 GeV. In the lowest pT-hat bin, 25 of 42 events in the Higgs mass window have at most one of the four leading jets matched to the hard scatter: they are made mostly of pileup jets, which is what the double-counting interpretation predicts. This is useful beyond this study: summing “hard scatter plus pileup” QCD samples over-counts low-HT crossings at high pileup. Code: norm_check.py.
